The OCS Standard

Our Premium Turnover Checklist

Every unit follows our meticulous protocol. Nothing overlooked, nothing left to chance.

Kitchen

  • Countertops, backsplash, sink, faucet, and disposal area
  • All cabinets, drawers, and shelving — inside and out
  • Refrigerator, stove/oven, microwave, and dishwasher — interior and exterior
  • Behind and underneath all pullable appliances
  • Range hood and filter cleaned
  • Sheila Shine applied to all stainless steel surfaces
  • Light fixtures and switch plates wiped
  • Floors swept, mopped, edges, corners, and baseboards detailed

Bathrooms

  • Tub, shower, and shower doors — tile, grout, and fixtures detailed
  • Toilet cleaned inside and out — bowl, base, tank, and behind
  • Sink, vanity, faucet, and mirror polished
  • All cabinets, drawers, and medicine cabinet — inside and out
  • Exhaust fan, light fixtures, switch plates, and towel racks
  • Chrome fixtures polished throughout
  • Floors swept, mopped, edges, corners, and baseboards detailed

Bedrooms & Living Areas

  • All windows cleaned — interior and exterior where accessible, including sills and tracks
  • Ceiling fans and blinds dusted and wiped
  • Fireplace exterior and interior cleaned where applicable
  • Closets — shelves, rods, and floors
  • Light fixtures, switches, and outlet covers cleaned
  • Doors and door frames wiped throughout
  • Floors swept, mopped, or vacuumed — carpet edging included

General & Additional

  • Washer/dryer and laundry area — including floors, bi-folds, and tracks
  • Water heater area, utility spaces, and bi-fold doors
  • Garage floors cleaned where applicable
  • Minor debris removal included with every turnover
  • Final walk-through quality inspection before unit is released
  • Rush and same-day turnover service available
  • Warranty cleans at no charge within 14 days
  • Excessive filth remediation with itemized pricing

Common Questions

What Property Managers Ask

Everything you need to know about partnering with OCS.

Are you fully insured and bonded?

Yes — Oakland Cleaning Services has carried general liability, commercial auto, and bonding coverage continuously since 1994. Certificates of insurance are provided to every property management company on request.

Can you provide a W-9 and meet vendor compliance requirements?

Absolutely. We supply W-9 forms, ACORD certificates, vendor onboarding packets, and any documentation your accounting team needs. Most management companies onboard us within a week.

What's your typical turnaround time?

We turn units quickly. In 30 years of business we've never missed a move-in date. Rush and same-day service is available for tight leasing timelines — we work to your schedule, not ours.

What's your warranty / re-clean policy?

Every unit is inspected before release. If anything misses our standard within 14 days, we re-clean at no charge. Our re-clean rate is consistently under 5%.

Do you require minimum commitments or long-term contracts?

We work from a written service agreement that protects both sides on rates and scope. No minimum unit count, no penalty for ending the relationship — though several of our core client partnerships have run for nearly all 30 years we've been in business.

How do you handle excessive filth or special situations?

Itemized surcharges based on a published schedule. Our crew documents conditions with photos and we communicate any extras before adding them to the invoice. No surprises.

Our Story

Built from the Ground Up

John Lanzetta grew up in Milford, Michigan. After graduating from Milford High School, he knew one thing for certain — he was going to build something to support his family. He made flyers, knocked on doors, and landed his first account at Hidden Lakes Apartments in Clarkston. It was just John and one other guy.

That was years before OCS was officially incorporated in 1994. Those early years were about learning the trade from the inside — understanding what property managers actually need, not what a sales pitch promises. Every unit cleaned, every callback handled, every relationship built one handshake at a time.

Three decades later, Oakland Cleaning Services is still family-owned and hands-on. John still walks properties. He still knows the maintenance techs, the leasing agents, and the porters by name — not just the managers. That's the difference between a vendor and a partner.

OCS has grown from one apartment complex to over 30 properties across Oakland and Macomb Counties, but the approach hasn't changed: show up, do the work right, and treat every property like your reputation depends on it — because it does.

John Lanzetta Founder & Owner, Oakland Cleaning Services, Inc.

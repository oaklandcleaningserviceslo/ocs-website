Privacy Policy

How Oakland Cleaning Services, Inc. collects, uses, and protects your information.

Effective July 11, 2026

Overview

Oakland Cleaning Services, Inc. ("OCS," "we," "us," or "our") is a commercial cleaning company based in Lake Orion, Michigan. We respect your privacy, and we keep this policy simple because our practices are simple. We collect only the information we need to serve you, and we do not sell it.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect personal information from our customers, prospective customers, and website visitors. It applies to information collected through our website (ocslakeorion.com), by phone, by email, and by text message. This policy is effective as of July 11, 2026.

Information We Collect

We collect contact details that you choose to provide to us, whether through our website quote form, by phone, by email, or by text message. This may include:

  • Your name and job title
  • Your phone number and email address
  • The name and location of your property, community, or business
  • Details about the cleaning services you are interested in

Like most websites, we also use Google Analytics to understand general website usage, such as which pages are visited. This information is collected through cookies and is not used by us to personally identify you.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and requests
  • Prepare and deliver quotes for cleaning services
  • Schedule, provide, and invoice for our services
  • Follow up on inquiries and completed work

We do not sell your information.

SMS / Text Messaging

Oakland Cleaning Services offers two-way, customer-initiated text messaging so that customers and prospective customers can reach us conveniently. Consumers who send a text message to our number, (248) 621-9775, are consenting to receive text message replies from Oakland Cleaning Services regarding their inquiry, quote, or service.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help, or contact us directly at (248) 343-2232 or oaklandcleaningserviceslo@gmail.com.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone. We share personal information only with service providers as needed to operate our business, such as the providers that process our website forms and deliver our email, and only to the extent necessary for them to perform those services on our behalf. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, we will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date.
